Scans directory or current drive for files that match given pattern

SYNTAX: 
    scan <pattern> OR scan <pattern> <dir>

    PATTERNS:
        '*' denotes that anything may takes its place (e.g 'ca*' = 'cat', 'car', 'carpet')
        '?' denotes that any single character may take its place (e.g 'ca?' = 'cat', 'car' BUT NOT 'carpet')


scan <pattern>:
    Scans the current drive for files that match the pattern.
    NOTE: This may be slower than just searching a specific directory

scan <pattern> <dir>:
    Scans the specified folder for files that match the pattern

To scan the 'C:/Dev' drive for C++ files,
    `scan *.cpp C:/Dev`
